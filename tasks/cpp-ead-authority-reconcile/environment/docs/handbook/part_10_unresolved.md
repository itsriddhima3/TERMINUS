## 24.1 Unresolved queue versus compliance bundle

Rows with missing creator, subject, or required place authority ids belong in
`/app/output/unresolved.jsonl`. The compliance bundle must still retain those
same rows for audit. Filtering unresolved collections out of `compliance.json`
while writing them to the unresolved queue violates batch QA policy from 2021
forward.
