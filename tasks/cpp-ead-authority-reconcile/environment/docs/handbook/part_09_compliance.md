## 23.4 Compliance bundle completeness

The `compliance.json` `records` array must contain every collection in the batch
regardless of per-field resolution status. The summary `unresolved_count` tracks
collections still missing required authority links; omitting rows from the
compliance bundle is invalid even when every entity has linked successfully.

## 23.5 Optional geographic linking

Collections with no `<geogname>` elements in `<controlaccess>` do not require
`place_names` authority ids for fully-linked status. Empty `place_names` is
expected when no geographic headings were declared in the finding aid.

## 23.6 Optional subject linking

Collections with no `<subject>` elements in `<controlaccess>` do not require
`subject_headings` authority ids for fully-linked status. Empty
`subject_headings` is expected when no topical headings were declared in the
finding aid.
